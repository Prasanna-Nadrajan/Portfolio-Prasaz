Sepsis is hardest to treat when it is caught late. Sentinal-AI reads multi-parameter ICU vitals and forecasts onset **48 hours** before clinical presentation, giving care teams a window to intervene.

<Tip variant="diamond" title="Built to be trusted">
  The pipeline is checked with automated Python AST inspection, so no future information leaks into training.
</Tip>

## Results

- **48 h** early warning window ahead of acute symptoms
- **Under 50 ms** inference latency from a containerised service
- **Zero data leakage**, verified by AST checks
- Decision thresholds tuned for high sensitivity while limiting alarm fatigue

## Pipeline

1. **Ingestion and ETL:** clean ICU vitals, handle irregular sampling and impute missing parameters
2. **Feature store:** physiological biomarkers, rolling vital trends, shock index and lab deltas
3. **Predictive core:** a sequential temporal deep network trained on clinical records
4. **Evaluation:** AUROC optimisation and risk threshold calibration
5. **Deployment:** a FastAPI microservice in Docker that delivers fast alerts to bedside monitors
