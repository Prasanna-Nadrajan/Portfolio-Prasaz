A machine learning pipeline that classifies land use and land cover from multi-spectral satellite imagery using ensemble methods.

## Why it matters

Accurate land-cover maps support environmental monitoring and urban zoning decisions.

## Stack

Python, Scikit-learn (Random Forest and SVM), QGIS and Google Earth Engine, on Sentinel-2 and Landsat-8 data.
